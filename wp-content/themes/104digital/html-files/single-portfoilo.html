Overview

Background

Threadly was created to help users effortlessly manage their personal wardrobe in a digital environment. As fashion collections grow, users struggle to track items, plan outfits, and maintain visibility across multiple storage locations. The goal was to design a clean, intuitive system that brings clarity and control to wardrobe management.

Problem

Users often face wardrobe overload — items get lost, underused, or forgotten. Existing solutions lack powerful organization tools, bulk management capabilities, and seamless photo processing. Assistants and stylists especially need faster ways to catalog and manage large clothing inventories.

Solution

We designed Threadly as a smart wardrobe ecosystem that combines AI-powered photo processing, advanced filtering, team permissions, and high-speed bulk management tools. The experience focuses on simplicity for end users while providing powerful backend capabilities for assistants and stylists.

Designing a seamless experience for wardrobe management

Threadly was designed to serve multiple user types including clients, stylists, and assistants. The challenge was balancing powerful inventory tools with a clean, approachable interface.

We focused heavily on reducing friction in three key areas:

  • Fast clothing upload and background removal
  • Smart categorization and filtering
  • Efficient team collaboration and permissions

The result is a system that feels lightweight for everyday users while remaining powerful enough for professional wardrobe teams managing thousands of items.
